CoCounsel Bench, or CoCoBench, is Thomson Reuters comprehensive benchmark for evaluating agentic legal AI. It is central to how we develop and assess CoCounsel Legal, giving our teams a consistent benchmark to measure against our Fiduciary-Grade AI™ standard. At Thomson Reuters, Fiduciary-Grade AI™ is our standard for how AI should work in high-stakes professions.
