*How we taught Claude to understand redlined legal alerts through dual-path prompts, XML preprocessing, and cognitive frameworks, validated by expert legal editors.* Imagine teaching an AI system to read legal documents the way an expert lawyer would — understanding not just what changed in a statute, but why it matters and which existing guidance needs updating. Now imagine that system missing 80% of the changes it was supposed to find. That was our starting point.
