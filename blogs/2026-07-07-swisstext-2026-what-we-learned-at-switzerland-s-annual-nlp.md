A full room for the TR Labs talk on evaluating agentic AI — Campus Oerlikon, University of Zurich. SwissText 2026 marked the 11th edition of the Swiss Text Analytics Conference, and the first time it was hosted at the University of Zurich (UZH), on the modern Campus Oerlikon, on 10 June 2026. The one-day summit brought together more than 100 researchers, engineers, and practitioners from across Switzerland and beyond for one of the region’s most focused gatherings on natural language processing, computational linguistics, and text analytics.
