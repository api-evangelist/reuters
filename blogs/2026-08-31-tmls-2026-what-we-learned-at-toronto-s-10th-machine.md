The Toronto Machine Learning Summit (TMLS) returned for its tenth edition on June 17–18, 2026, held at CIBC Square in downtown Toronto. TMLS’s focus on applied AI brings together practitioners, researchers and leaders from industry to share progress and lessons learned.
